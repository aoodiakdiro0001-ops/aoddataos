# START HERE — Aodı Akdıro Data OS (5 minutes)

## 1) Open the app
Double-click **`app/index.html`**. It runs offline in any browser. Nothing to install.

## 2) Your first week (short version)
- **Day moves:** 🗓️ 21-Day Plan → record Day 1 (source + owner inventory) and keep the chain going.
- **Choose tools:** 🗃️ Tool Map → compare candidates per need, pick one, freeze it for the week.
- **Use contracts:** 📜 Data Contracts → copy one, fill `{{variables}}`, ship the exact output format.
- **Watch cost:** 🧮 Cost Calculator → storage × price + scans × price + seats → know your biggest driver.
- **Gate everything:** 🛡️ Governance Policy → the high-risk items before any public-facing data.

## 3) Progress saves itself
localStorage (`aos_aoddataos_*`) — export JSON from the quickbar or per-module CSVs from `export/`.

## 4) If anything breaks
Re-open `app/index.html`, or rebuild: `python3 build.py` (auditor `python3 check.py` must print CLEAN).
